# Zeylora AI Brand Identity System

Zeylora AI is a premium AI photo editing SaaS for creators, professionals, founders, marketers, ecommerce operators, and everyday users who want visual results that feel polished, fast, and trustworthy.

The brand should feel like a modern funded AI startup, not a generic AI tool template.

## 1. Brand Direction

### Brand Personality

- Premium, calm, intelligent, and visually confident.
- Futuristic without feeling cold or confusing.
- Elegant and cinematic rather than loud or gimmicky.
- Creative-professional, not toy-like.
- Trustworthy enough for payments, uploads, and personal photos.
- Ambitious and global, with room to expand into many AI visual tools.

### Visual Tone

- Dark luxury base.
- Controlled cinematic gradients.
- Sharp typographic hierarchy.
- Glass surfaces used sparingly.
- Subtle glow as a signal of AI processing, not decoration everywhere.
- High contrast, high readability, clean spacing.

### Emotional Feeling

Users should feel:

- "This looks powerful."
- "My photo will be handled professionally."
- "This is not a cheap AI website."
- "I understand what to do immediately."
- "I want to upload a photo and see the result."

### Target Audience Feeling

Zeylora AI should speak to:

- Creators who care about aesthetic output.
- Professionals who need better profile/product/marketing images.
- Small business owners who want fast visual upgrades.
- Agencies and marketers who may later buy larger credit packs.
- Global paid-traffic users who need clarity, trust, and fast action.

### UI/UX Design Philosophy

- Conversion first, but never visually cheap.
- Upload action should always feel close and obvious.
- The product should show visual confidence quickly.
- The interface should reduce uncertainty around credits, previews, downloads, and result quality.
- Advanced architecture should stay invisible to users but clear inside admin.
- Avoid long decorative sections before the primary upload action.
- Use motion to clarify state changes, not to distract.

## 2. Logo System

### Logo Concept

Zeylora AI should use a custom abstract "Z aperture" mark:

- The mark combines the letter Z, a camera aperture, and an AI light beam.
- It should feel like visual technology, not a sparkle icon.
- The shape should scale down well for favicon/app icon use.
- It should be memorable enough to stand beside modern SaaS brands.

### Primary Logo

Primary lockup:

- Icon mark on the left.
- Wordmark: "ZEYLORA" in strong uppercase.
- Smaller "AI" capsule or suffix.
- Best used in navbar, footer, auth screens, invoices, and legal pages.

Recommended proportions:

- Icon size: 1x.
- Wordmark height: about 0.42x of icon height.
- AI suffix: about 0.30x of icon height.
- Gap between mark and wordmark: 0.28x.

### Icon Mark

Use the icon mark alone for:

- Favicon.
- App icon.
- Loading states.
- Social avatar.
- Mobile compressed header.
- Watermark concept.

Icon rules:

- Do not place the mark inside random circles unless the surface requires an app-icon container.
- Do not add cheap sparkle symbols around it.
- Do not stretch or rotate the mark.
- Do not use low-contrast gradients on bright backgrounds.

### Favicon Concept

Favicon should use only the mark:

- Dark rounded-square background.
- Z aperture symbol in cyan-to-violet gradient.
- Optional inner light point.
- Must remain readable at 16px and 32px.

### Monochrome Versions

Use monochrome versions when:

- Printed material.
- Legal/payment documents.
- Very small UI surfaces.
- Light backgrounds where gradient is too complex.

Versions:

- White mark on dark.
- Near-black mark on light.
- Single-color cyan mark for special UI states.

### Dark/Light Compatibility

Dark mode:

- Primary gradient mark.
- White wordmark.
- AI suffix in cyan/violet capsule.

Light mode:

- Obsidian wordmark.
- Dark mark or controlled gradient mark.
- Avoid glowing effects on pure white.

### Logo Spacing Rules

Minimum clear space:

- Around full logo: at least the width of the icon's inner light point multiplied by 4.
- Around icon mark: at least 20% of icon width.

Minimum sizes:

- Full logo digital: 120px wide minimum.
- Icon mark digital: 24px minimum.
- Favicon: 16px supported, 32px preferred.

### Logo Usage Rules

Do:

- Use on dark, clean surfaces.
- Keep enough contrast.
- Use the icon mark consistently.
- Use gradient version for hero/header/product identity.

Do not:

- Add sparkles, emojis, clipart, or generic AI magic icons.
- Use many gradients inside the wordmark.
- Put logo on noisy images without a dark overlay.
- Apply heavy shadows to the wordmark.

## 4. Typography System

### Font Direction

Recommended production font pairing:

- Heading/UI: `Inter`, `Geist Sans`, or `Satoshi`.
- Body: same family for consistency.
- Numeric/dashboard data: `Inter` or `Geist Mono` only where useful.

Preferred if available:

- Primary: Geist Sans.
- Fallback: Inter, system UI.

### Heading Style

- Strong, compact, confident.
- Font weight: 800-900.
- Letter spacing: 0.
- Avoid negative tracking.
- Use large type only in real hero sections.

Suggested scale:

- Hero H1 desktop: 64-72px.
- Hero H1 mobile: 40-44px.
- Section H2 desktop: 44-52px.
- Section H2 mobile: 30-36px.
- Card heading: 18-22px.

### Body Style

- Body: 16-18px for marketing.
- Dashboard/admin body: 14-16px.
- Line height: 1.55-1.75 for prose.
- Muted copy should remain readable, not too dim.

### UI Font Hierarchy

- Eyebrow labels: 12px, 700-800, uppercase only when short.
- Button text: 14px, 700-800.
- Form labels: 13px, 700.
- Metadata: 12px, 600-700.
- Credit/package numbers: 36-52px, 850-900.

### Spacing Rhythm

Base rhythm:

- 4px micro.
- 8px small.
- 12px compact.
- 16px default component gap.
- 24px section internal gap.
- 40px section heading-to-grid gap.
- 72-96px desktop section spacing.
- 56-72px mobile section spacing.

## 5. UI Design Language

### Buttons

Primary:

- Rounded pill.
- Cyan or primary gradient.
- Dark text for cyan button.
- Strong hover lift.
- Subtle glow only on primary CTA.

Secondary:

- Transparent/glass.
- White border at 10-16% opacity.
- White text.
- Hover background slightly stronger.

Danger/destructive:

- Rose text or border.
- Avoid strong red fills unless final destructive confirmation.

### Cards

Marketing cards:

- Dark glass surface.
- 1px subtle border.
- 20-28px radius.
- Hover: slight lift, brighter border, deeper shadow.

Dashboard/admin cards:

- More restrained.
- Less glow.
- Denser spacing.
- Clear scan hierarchy.

### Glow Usage

Use glow to indicate:

- Primary CTA.
- Upload area.
- Active AI processing.
- Selected pricing package.
- Logo mark.

Do not glow every card or text block.

### Borders

- Default border: `rgba(255,255,255,0.10)`.
- Active border: cyan at 30-40%.
- Error border: rose at 35%.
- Success border: emerald at 25%.

### Shadows

- Use deep black shadows for cinematic depth.
- Avoid flat template-style drop shadows.
- Keep dashboards calmer than marketing sections.

### Gradients

Gradients should feel like light moving through glass:

- Cyan to violet to magenta.
- Mostly used in backgrounds, logo, active states.
- Do not use rainbow or overly saturated gradients.

### Hover Effects

- Small lift: `translateY(-2px to -4px)`.
- Border brightening.
- Icon background transition.
- No aggressive scaling.

### Animation Direction

- Slow, subtle, premium.
- Fade up for section reveal.
- Gentle shimmer for upload/processing.
- Float only on major hero visual.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Glassmorphism Usage

Use glass for:

- Hero upload card.
- Before/after preview overlay.
- Pricing cards.
- Feature cards.

Avoid glass for:

- Long tables.
- Legal pages.
- Dense admin data.

### Radius System

- Buttons: 999px.
- Small chips: 999px.
- Inputs: 14-16px.
- Cards: 20-28px.
- Hero/upload containers: 28-32px.
- Dashboard tables: 12-16px.

### Icon Style

- Use Lucide-style line icons.
- Stroke width: 2-2.5.
- Icons should support labels, not replace unclear actions.
- Avoid playful icons for core AI/product/payment actions.

## 7. Homepage Art Direction

### Hero Style

- Full dark cinematic background.
- Strong headline with one gradient phrase.
- Upload CTA visible immediately.
- Trust chips below CTA.
- Hero visual should be the upload/result workflow, not abstract decoration.

### Upload Area Style

- Large glass drop zone.
- Dashed or illuminated border.
- Primary button with high contrast.
- Clear file rules and credit context.
- Later: show selected tool, estimated credits, preview status, and unlock action.

### Before/After Presentation

- Large cinematic comparison panel.
- Strong before/after labels.
- Slider handle should feel premium.
- Use real result examples when available.
- Avoid stock-photo atmosphere that hides actual result quality.

### Trust Sections

Should communicate:

- Secure uploads.
- Refund-safe failed jobs.
- Clear credit usage.
- Payment/legal readiness.
- Provider fallback and uptime mindset.

### Pricing Direction

- 3 credit packs first.
- One recommended pack.
- Explain credits simply.
- Avoid subscription complexity until ready.
- Keep checkout CTA clear and early.

### Dashboard Direction

- Quiet, practical, premium.
- Less marketing glow.
- Clear credit balance.
- Recent jobs.
- Generated images.
- Download links.
- Payment/credit history.
- Failed/refunded status clarity.

## 9. Conversion and Trust Rules

Always keep:

- Upload CTA visible early.
- Pricing understandable.
- Legal/support links accessible.
- AI-result expectations honest.
- Preview/download flow clear.
- Credit usage visible before processing.
- Failed job/refund logic clear.
- Mobile layout tight and action-oriented.

Avoid:

- Overly long hero copy.
- Too many decorative sections.
- Fake guarantees.
- Generic AI sparkle branding.
- Low contrast muted text.
- Heavy animation on mobile.

## 10. Brand Summary

Zeylora AI should feel like:

> A cinematic AI photo studio for global creators and professionals, built with the polish of a funded SaaS startup and the operational maturity of a real credit-based product.
